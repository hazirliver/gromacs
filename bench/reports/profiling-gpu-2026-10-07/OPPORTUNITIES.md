# GPU kernel and GPU-side code-path opportunities: MAS1 on one L40S

Baseline: production command on the production-like P build, 0.7428 ms/step [0.7417, 0.7440] = 232.6
ns/day, 113.5 kJ GPU energy per simulated ns (`raw/exp/gpukern-e2e3`, P-t16; day 1: 0.7417). "Share" = the
part of the step time the mechanism lives in. Upper bounds are measured (timing-only skips or
knock-outs with the real concurrency); measured gains come from the throwaway worktree
`gromacs-exp-gpukern` (patch `bench/profiling/experiments/exp-gpukern.patch`), every change a runtime
switch in one binary, 5 seeded interleaved rounds against P. Energy model as day 1: under the binding
325 W cap, work reductions lower energy per ns at least as much as time.

Prior art: `raw/prior-art-gpu.md` (GitLab, checked today) and day 1's `raw/prior-art.md`.
